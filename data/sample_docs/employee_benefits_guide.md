# Employee Benefits and Leave Policy Guide

## 1. Healthcare, Dental, and Vision Coverage
The company provides comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage starting on the first day of employment.
- **Medical Plans**: Employees may choose between the High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with an employer-funded HSA ($1,500 individual / $3,000 family) or the PPO plan.
- **Premium Subsidies**: The company covers 100% of employee medical premiums and 80% of eligible dependent premiums.
- **Wellness Stipend**: Employees receive a $150 monthly wellness reimbursement usable for gym memberships, fitness trackers, or mental health apps.

## 2. Retirement and 401(k) Matching
- **401(k) Provider**: Fidelity Investments.
- **Company Match**: 100% match on the first 4% of eligible salary contributed, plus 50% match on the next 2% (maximum effective match of 5%).
- **Vesting Schedule**: Employer matching funds vest immediately at 100% with no waiting period.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) and Parental Leave
- **Flexible PTO**: Salaried full-time employees operate on a flexible, trust-based PTO policy with a recommended minimum of 20 days off per year.
- **Company Holidays**: 12 standard paid company holidays plus 2 floating cultural holidays.
- **Parental Leave**: 16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave for all new parents (birth, adoption, or foster placement) after 6 months of continuous employment.
- **Bereavement Leave**: Up to 5 consecutive days of paid leave for the loss of an immediate family member.
