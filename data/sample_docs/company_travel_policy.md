Overview:
This document describes the company's travel and expense policy for employees
traveling domestically and internationally on approved business trips.

Booking Travel:
All flights and hotels must be booked through the approved corporate travel
portal. Employees may book economy class for flights under 6 hours and
premium economy for flights exceeding 6 hours. Business class requires
director-level approval.

Expense Reimbursement:
Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of trip completion.
Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Meals are reimbursed up to
$75 per day domestically and $100 per day internationally.

Approval Workflow:
Trips under $2,000 total cost require manager approval only. Trips exceeding
$2,000 require both manager and department head approval before booking.

Non-Reimbursable Items:
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and expenses for travel
companions who are not employees are not reimbursable under this policy.
